# Google Form: BharatGPT Citizen Survey (target: 50+ responses)
Set every question to "Required". Export responses as CSV -> survey/survey_responses.csv

1. Age group (Under 18 / 18-30 / 31-45 / 46-60 / 60+)
2. Location type (Urban / Semi-urban / Rural)
3. Education (Up to 10th / 12th / Graduate / Postgraduate)
4. Preferred language for government information (English / Hindi / Kannada / Tamil / Telugu / Other)
5. Have you faced difficulty finding information about government schemes or services? (Yes / Sometimes / No)
6. How do you usually find such information? (Government portal / Agent or middleman / Relative or friend / Helpline / WhatsApp or social media)
7. Have you ever filed a government grievance? (Yes, resolved on time / Yes, delayed or not resolved / No)
8. How likely are you to use a chatbot in your own language? (1 to 5)
9. Preferred input (Text / Voice / Both)
10. Biggest concern about such a chatbot (Wrong answers / Privacy / Language support / Ease of use)
